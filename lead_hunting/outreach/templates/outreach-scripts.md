# Outreach Templates — CoreTrust Web/App Dev

## Template 1: Cold Call — Business Without Website

"Hey, is this [Business Name]? My name's Trevon, I'm local here in Brampton. I noticed when I searched for [their niche] in [their city] on Google, you didn't come up with a website. I help local businesses get found online — would you be open to a quick 10-minute chat about what that could look like for you? No pressure at all."

**If they say yes:** Book a 15-min call or in-person visit. Bring a mockup.
**If they say no:** "Totally understand. If you ever need help with that, I'm easy to reach." Leave your name.

## Audit Checklist (Use This for Free Audits)

When you find a lead, audit their current presence and use this as your conversation starter:

- [ ] Google Business Profile: Claimed? Complete? Photos? Hours? Reviews?
- [ ] Website: Exists? Mobile-friendly? Load speed? SSL? Contact info visible?
- [ ] SEO: Do they rank for "[their service] [their city]"? What position?
- [ ] Social: Active? Consistent? Linking to website?
- [ ] Reviews: Google review count vs. competitors? Rating?
- [ ] Competitors: What are the top 3 competitors doing that they're not?

**The audit IS the pitch.** Show them the gaps. The sale makes itself.
